Paint mixes differently from light.

A screen mixes two colours by averaging their light. Paint works the other way round: each pigment absorbs part of the light, and you see only what both of them leave behind.

The paint here is mixed with Kubelka–Munk theory, which models how a layer of paint absorbs and scatters light.
