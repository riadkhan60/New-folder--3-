Grinding alone gives a greyish blue.

Around 1400 the Tuscan painter Cennino Cennini described how to get the blue out of the stone, and leave everything else behind.

  1. 1

    Grind

    Grind the lapis to a powder on the slab, as you just did.

  2. 2

    Knead

    Knead the powdered stone into a dough of wax, resin and oil, and let it rest.

  3. 3

    Wash out

    Knead the dough in lye. The blue washes out and sinks to the bottom of the bowl; the calcite, pyrite and other impurities stay in the dough.

The first washings give the finest blue. Later ones are paler, down to the last: a pale grey-blue called ultramarine ash.
