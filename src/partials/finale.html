10,935m 10,935 metres: the floor of the Challenger Deep

You are on the floor of the Challenger Deep, the deepest point on Earth. Outside the sphere, about 1,100 bar of water presses on 90 mm of titanium. It is 2.5 °C, and the only light is the light we brought.
