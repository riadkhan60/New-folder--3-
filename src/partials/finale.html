10,935m 10,935 metres: the floor of the Challenger Deep

You are on the floor of the Challenger Deep, the deepest point on Earth. Outside the viewport the water presses on Vesper at about 1,100 bar, a little over a tonne on every square centimetre. It is 2.5 °C, and the only light is the light we brought.

Reserve a dive

Six guests a season come this far. Tell us who is coming and when you would like to go, and a dive planner will take it from there.

Nothing is charged now. Dives leave Apra Harbor, Guam, between November and April. Every guest completes a short medical questionnaire before a seat is confirmed.

Prices are per guest, for the whole trip.
