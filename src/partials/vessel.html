Vesper

A titanium sphere 1.5 metres across, wrapped in buoyant foam and fitted with ten thrusters. Vesper takes two guests and a pilot to any depth in the ocean, then brings them home.

  1. Drawn

    Everything starts with the sphere. The rest of Vesper is drawn around it.

  2. Built

    Foam made of hollow glass beads gives her lift. Titanium and acrylic keep the pressure out.

  3. Lit

    Twenty-two lamps come on, one bank at a time. Down here, nothing else makes white light.

  • Titanium pressure sphere 90 mm wall, 1.5 m inside
  • Rated to 11,000 m full ocean depth
  • Syntactic foam hollow glass beads in resin
  • Three viewports acrylic, 180 mm thick
  • 22 lamps white for filming, red for watching
