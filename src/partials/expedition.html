Nine days, one dive

The Hadal expedition sails from Apra Harbor, Guam, to the Challenger Deep and back. Almost all of it is preparation for twelve hours under water.

Below 6,000 metres, Vesper switches to red observation lamps. Most deep-sea animals cannot see red light, so we can watch them without blinding them.

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Guam

    Apra Harbor, Guam

    Fly in and settle by the harbor. In the evening you meet your pilot, the ship’s captain and the science team over dinner.

  2. Day 2

    Board MV Lantern

    Under way, heading south-west

    Lantern leaves Apra Harbor in the morning and sails about 300 km south-west, to the southern end of the Mariana Trench. She reaches the dive site overnight.

  3. Day 3

    Inside the sphere

    On station above the Challenger Deep

    You climb into Vesper’s titanium sphere for the first time: 1.5 m across inside, three viewports, two guests and a pilot. Then emergency drills, and sea trials alongside the ship.

  4. Day 4

    A first dive to 1,000 m

    On station

    The Twilight profile, as practice. You learn the rhythm of the cabin and watch the blue outside the viewports fade to black long before you reach the bottom.

  5. Day 5

    Weather and sonar

    On station

    The crew reads the swell forecast and picks the day. Lantern’s multibeam sonar maps the target on the trench floor, and you plan the route across it with your pilot.

  6. Day 6

    The descent

    11°22′ N, 142°35′ E

    Vesper leaves the surface at first light. About four hours down, up to four hours on the floor of the Challenger Deep, about four hours back up. Outside the sphere the water is 2.5 °C, at 1,100 bar.

    On the floor, Vesper is 10,935 metres down.

  7. Day 7

    Rest and review

    On station

    A slow morning. In the afternoon you sit with the science team and go through the footage and the data from your dive.

  8. Day 8

    Back to Guam

    Under way, heading north-east

    Lantern makes the crossing back to Apra Harbor. Most guests spend it on deck, looking at the water they were under two days before.

  9. Day 9

    Farewell

    Apra Harbor, Guam

    A last dinner with the crew, then flights home. You leave with your dive log, signed by your pilot.
