Calibre R‑01 · the going train

Five beats a second.

The mainspring pushes the wheels. The escapement lets them move one tooth at a time, and the balance decides how often. Slow it down to see a single beat, or speed it up and watch the spring run down.

Finishing

Finished where no one looks.

Every part is decorated by hand, including the faces you never see once the watch is closed. Move across each sample to turn it in the light.

  • Côtes de Genève

    Stripes cut across the plate with a turning wooden wheel and abrasive paste.

    3 h a plate

  • Perlage

    Overlapping circles, pressed one at a time with a spinning peg.

    1,400 circles

  • Anglage

    Every edge filed to 45°, then polished with gentian wood until it mirrors.

    40 h a movement

  • Bleui

    Screws laid on hot brass filings until the steel turns cornflower blue.

    290 °C
