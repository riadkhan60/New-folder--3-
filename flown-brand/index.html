Usage

Clear spaceKeep space equal to the height of the wordmark's "o" (about 40% of the mark's height) on every side.
Minimum sizeHorizontal logo: 96px / 25mm wide. Mark: 16px - use the small-size icon art below 48px.
BackgroundsGradient mark on Ink or white. On photos or busy colour, use the solid white or black version.
Don'tRecolour the blades, rotate or skew the mark, add effects, or re-set the wordmark in another typeface.
